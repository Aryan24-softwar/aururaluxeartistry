About The Artist

The Art of Timeless, Luminous Elegance

Hello, I’m Elena Roche, founder and lead artist of Aurora Luxe Artistry. With over eight years perfecting the craft across London, Paris, and New York fashion weeks as well as hundreds of intimate luxury weddings, I have dedicated my career to making every client feel radiant, confident, and utterly unforgettable.

“My philosophy is simple: Makeup should never be a mask. It is a refined celebration of your unique contours, glowing skin, and inner grace.”

Specializing in high-definition bridal styling, flawless airbrush skin finishes, and red-carpet glam, I combine dermatological skin preparation techniques with world-class cosmetic formulations to ensure your makeup lasts flawlessly for over 16 hours through tears, hugs, and high-energy celebrations.
